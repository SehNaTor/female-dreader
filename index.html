Meet Mariam Oriyomi Adeoye

Passionate Hairstylist, Beauty Enthusiast, and Founder of Female Dreader.

Who I Am

My name is Mariam Oriyomi Adeoye, a professional hairstylist and the founder of Female Dreader. My journey in the beauty industry is fueled by a deep passion for beauty, creativity, and aesthetics. Through years of dedication and continuous learning, I have developed expertise in dreadlocks, braiding, stitch braids, wig installation, and modern hairstyling techniques.

Beyond hairstyling, I am also a student of the Department of Public Administration at the National Open University of Nigeria. Balancing academics and entrepreneurship has strengthened my commitment to excellence, discipline, and personal growth.

My mission is to help every client look and feel confident through quality hairstyling services tailored to their individual style and personality.

Highlights & Achievements

  • Founder and CEO of Female Dreader
  • Skilled in dreadlock installation and maintenance
  • Experienced in braiding and stitch braid styling
  • Dedicated to client satisfaction and hair care excellence
  • Combining academic excellence with entrepreneurship
